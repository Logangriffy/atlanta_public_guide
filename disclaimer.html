A few important notes about the guide.

Atlanta Local is independently published by Logan Griffiths. The goal is to make local information and recommendations useful while being clear about changing information and compensated relationships.

Independent publication

References to businesses, brands, products, organizations or other third parties do not imply sponsorship, endorsement or partnership unless that relationship is specifically disclosed.

Changing local information

Hours, prices, routes, availability, business details and other local information can change. Verify time-sensitive details with the relevant business, municipality, school district, utility, map provider or other authoritative source before relying on them.

Recommendations and listings

Inclusion in the directory does not automatically mean that Logan personally recommends or has visited a place. When content reflects a personal recommendation or first-hand experience, the wording will aim to make that distinction clear.

Referral, affiliate and sponsored relationships

Some recommendations may include affiliate links, referral codes, sponsorships or other compensated relationships. When a relationship may provide Logan with a financial or other material benefit, it will be disclosed near the relevant content or link.

Third-party links

Atlanta Local may link to external websites, maps, businesses or services. Those destinations are operated by third parties and may have their own terms, privacy practices and content. A link is provided for convenience and does not make Atlanta Local responsible for the third-party site.

Privacy

Atlanta Local does not currently offer user accounts, checkout or a public contact form. If you follow a link to another platform or service, information you provide there is governed by that service’s privacy practices. Any future feature that collects information directly on Atlanta Local should be accompanied by an updated privacy notice explaining what is collected and why.
