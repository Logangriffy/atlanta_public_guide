ABOUT LOGAN

The person behind Atlanta Local.

Atlanta Local is my independent personal project: part local guide, part relocation resource and part home for the things I actually use and recommend.

Why I built this

I wanted one place to keep the useful things I learn while exploring Atlanta and North Georgia. The goal is simple: make it easier to find good local places, understand different cities and get oriented if you are new to the area.

A personal brand, not an employer website

This site is built around my broader personal brand and is designed to remain useful regardless of where I work. My career may show up in my personal content from time to time, but Atlanta Local itself is not an official website for any employer, homebuilder or real-estate company.

What belongs here

Local recommendations, city guides, moving resources, Tesla and EV content, tech, products I genuinely use and other topics that fit the broader personal brand. The common thread is that I should have a real reason for putting something here.

Transparency

If I eventually use affiliate links, referral codes, sponsorships or other compensated relationships, I’ll disclose them where they appear. Local listings are not endorsements unless I explicitly say they are.
