ABOUT LOGAN

The person behind Atlanta Local.

I built Atlanta Local to make it easier to discover good places, understand the area and keep track of the recommendations I would actually share with friends.

Why I built this guide

Atlanta and North Georgia cover a huge area, and useful local information is often scattered across dozens of websites, maps and social posts. I wanted one place where I could organize what I find and make it easier to explore a city, plan a weekend or get oriented after a move.

What I cover

The guide focuses on restaurants, coffee, parks, activities, shopping, city guides and practical moving resources. Logan’s Picks adds another side of the site with Tesla and EV ownership, tech, everyday gear and other products I have a real reason to recommend.

How I choose what to include

Some places come from personal experience and others are added because they are useful, notable or worth researching. I try to keep the distinction clear. The goal is not to rank everything or pretend I have personally visited every listing. It is to build a useful starting point and keep improving it over time.

Recommendations & transparency

When I make a personal recommendation, I want it to be clear why I like it and who I think it is useful for. If a recommendation includes an affiliate link, referral benefit, sponsorship or other compensated relationship, that relationship will be disclosed near the relevant content.
