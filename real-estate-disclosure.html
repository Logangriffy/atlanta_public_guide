REAL ESTATE DISCLOSURE

Atlanta Local is currently an informational guide.

This page explains how real-estate-related content is handled before any brokerage-approved lead or contact features are added.

License status

Logan Griffiths is a Georgia-licensed real estate salesperson.

What this site currently does

Atlanta Local provides local information, city guides and general relocation education.

What this site currently does not do

Atlanta Local does not currently display homes for sale, live builder inventory, builder pricing or incentives, or offer brokerage services through the website.

Property-specific information

School assignments, utilities, taxes, boundaries and similar details can depend on the exact address and should be verified with the appropriate official source.

Future real-estate features

Any future real-estate contact or lead-generation feature will be added only after the required brokerage approval, supervision and disclosures are in place.
