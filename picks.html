Tech & everyday gear

Good recommendations should be specific. I use this section for devices, tools and everyday gear that I have enough experience with to explain what I like, what I would change and whether I would buy it again.

Local favorites vs. the directory

The local directory can include places that are useful, notable or worth researching even if I have not personally visited them. A local favorite should have a stronger personal reason behind it. That distinction keeps the guide useful without pretending every listing is a firsthand review.

How recommendations work

I do not want this page to become a wall of generic product links. Items are included because I think they are useful or interesting. Some links may be affiliate or referral links, and when that applies the relationship is disclosed near the link.

What I will not do

I will not label something as personally tested when it was not, hide a compensated relationship, or keep a recommendation up just because it once worked well. Products, services and offers change, so this page should evolve with them.
