BEFORE YOU MOVE

Five things worth checking early.

01

Commute & transportation

Test the actual trips that matter to you at the times you would normally make them. Consider driving routes, transit access, airport trips and how different corridors behave during peak periods.

02

Utilities

Electric, water, sewer, gas, trash and internet providers can change by address, municipality or service territory. Verify each provider for the exact property rather than assuming a city has one universal setup.

03

Schools

School assignments can depend on the exact address and boundaries can change. Use the appropriate school district’s official address lookup or enrollment office instead of relying on a neighborhood name or third-party listing.

04

Georgia driver & vehicle setup

New Georgia residents have state requirements for transferring a driver’s license and registering vehicles. Check the current DDS and Department of Revenue instructions before your move date.

05

Healthcare & everyday services

Map the things you use regularly: primary care, urgent care, pharmacy, grocery stores, pet care, gyms and other routines. A location can feel very different once you look beyond the weekend attractions.

A BETTER FIRST VISIT

Spend a day living your normal routine here.

Instead of only touring the obvious attractions, drive the commute you would actually make, stop at the grocery store you would probably use, grab coffee nearby, visit a park or gym, and check the route home after work. That kind of ordinary day can tell you more about an area than a polished weekend itinerary.
